Flagship DSP Modules

TESSERACT

Reverse Granular Cascade
TOGGLE MODULE

A dual-windowed reverse granular engine. Tesseract silently records incoming audio into a hidden buffer and plays back overlapping 500ms audio grains crossfaded with mathematical Hann envelopes. Integrated with a 300Hz high-pass filter, it creates eerie, tension-building reverse sweeps with zero low-end mud.

Dual Read-Heads • Zero-Click Hann Windowing
◇

HOLOGRAM

Pitch-Tracked Resonator
TOGGLE MODULE

Pitch-tracked sympathetic resonance that converts atonal noise, percussive clicks, or foley into singing musical overtones. Tracks incoming live MIDI note frequency in real-time, applying a 1.5Hz stereo offset between left and right channels for 3D crystalline glass textures.

Real-Time MIDI Tracking • Psychoacoustic Stereo Beating

Pristine DSP Arsenal

The FX Matrix

VOID

De-Cluttered Reverb Space

Massive 3D space algorithm featuring a built-in 180Hz high-pass filter in the feedback loop. High frequencies wash into atmosphere while bass transient punch remains intact.

MIRAGE

Liquid Parallel Phaser

Cascaded 6-stage liquid all-pass phaser matrix. Parallel wet/dry balancing provides smooth underwater movement without harsh metallic ringing or volume spikes.

PRISM

Haas Micro-Widener

Operating in the 8ms Haas delay zone with a 300Hz high-pass filter. Expands stereo width beyond the speakers while eliminating comb-filtering and center mud.

QUANTUM FLUX

Tape Wow & Saturation

Simulates physical tape machine wow, flutter, and non-linear hyperbolic tangent saturation. Injects warmth and subtle analog instability into digital audio.

GRAVITY

Sub-Harmonic Density

A specialized low-pass smoothing filter that grounds audio signals, giving light plucks and thin samples heavy, cinematic body and low-frequency weight.

ASTRAL

Stereo Spatial Panner

Sine-modulated auto-panning engine that moves sound sources across the left and right panoramic channels to create immersive spatial movement.

HALO

Cascading Dual Delay

Offset dual-channel delay buffer providing cross-feedback echoes that wrap around central mono signals for spacious, ethereal trails.

PULSE

Rhythmic Tremolo Engine

Adjustable amplitude modulation engine that carves rhythmic gating patterns into static drones and pads to match project energy.

FILTER & LFO

Cubic-Curve Filter Sweep

Resonant filter curve preserving high-frequency clarity down to 70% cutoff, controlled by LFO Rate and Depth for sweeping filter modulation.
